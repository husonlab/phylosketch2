# PhyloSketch 2.4.4

## Bug fixes

- **Export Newick** in the export button of the tool bar (previously labelled "Export data...") did nothing. It now
  exports the phylogeny in Newick format, as File > Export > Newick does
- In Move mode, moving a node no longer opens the selection rectangle. This happened on touch screens when the
  finger went down on the edge of a node
- Capturing an image that is unsuitable, because too much of it is dark, is reported as a message that says what
  is wrong with the image, and no longer as an exception in the log
- In resize mode, redo of a scale no longer leaves the scaled edges without a path, so that they were no longer
  drawn
- With the grid on, a drag that is too short to take a node to the next grid point no longer adds an empty step
  to the undo list
- In resize mode, a press on the selection rectangle or on its handle that moves nothing no longer adds an empty
  step to the undo list, and no longer empties the redo list
- In resize mode, a scale made with shift held down can always be redone. It could not be redone when the last
  step of the drag changed nothing
- **Capture > Load Image...** is available in every mode. It was greyed out in View and in Move mode, and so in
  every document that had just been opened. If the document is not empty, the image is loaded into a new
  window, as before

## Capture

- Capture recognizes more leaf labels. Where a branch runs right up to its label, the end of the branch was read
  as a dash in front of the label, and the label was then left out. Such a label is now read again without the
  piece of branch
- Capture works on grey pictures, on photographs that are lit unevenly, on pictures with a transparent
  background and on drawings in which clades lie on a coloured background. What is drawn is now told from the
  background around it, no longer by one level of brightness for the whole picture. A picture of light lines on
  a dark background is captured as well
- Dashed and dotted edges are captured as edges. Where a dashed edge passes over another edge, or two edges of
  different colour pass straight through each other, the two cross: no node is put there. A dashed edge may end
  in an arrowhead
- Text no longer cuts edges. Only the ink of a word is erased from the picture, not the whole box that the
  text recognition reports for it, which often overlaps a branch next to the word, and which is sometimes
  reported where there is no text at all, over part of the tree. What is left of a letter that touches a
  line, and the frame around a label, are removed
- Fewer nodes and edges from what is not part of the phylogeny. Small pieces on their own are dropped, and so
  are lines that are not connected to the root, such as a scale bar, a bracket or the frame of a legend, unless
  they look like a tree of their own. This is the new option *removeStrayLines* in the capture parameters,
  which is on; with it off, all lines are kept as before
- More labels are kept: a label that ends in a closing bracket or quotation mark that was opened in it, such as
  "Ocelot (Brazil)", or in the full stop of an abbreviation, was left out, as was a label that stands in a
  dashed line. A word next to a node that is not a leaf becomes the label of that node. Below a tree with the
  root at the top, labels that stand side by side in one line are no longer joined into one
- When a picture is loaded and nothing has been drawn or captured yet, the root marker is shown by itself. It
  is put where the root is likely to be, with the direction in which the tree is likely to grow, and the picture
  is scrolled so that the marker can be seen. Until now *Place Root* had to be used, the marker appeared at the
  top left corner of the picture, and it had to be dragged to the root
- The root marker is a ring with an arrow that points the way the tree grows, in the accent colour of the
  program, no longer in red. It keeps its size on the screen when the picture is zoomed: zoomed out far enough
  to see a large picture, it used to be too small to read or to touch
- Dropped near a line, the root marker takes that line, and its ring is filled while it is on one. Capture
  needs the root on a line: a few pixels beside it, the root was left without an edge
- A bar at the top of the window shows the direction and a **Capture** button, which can be pressed while the
  marker is on a line. The directions are called *Left to right*, *Top to bottom*, *Right to left*, *Bottom to
  top* and *Radial*, in the bar and in the menu, which is now **Direction** (it was *Root Side*, with Left,
  Top, Right, Bottom and Center)
- A capture is one step in the undo list and ends in Sketch mode, in which the phylogeny is completed by hand;
  the picture stays in the background. Undo takes the capture back and returns to Capture mode, with the marker
  where it was. Until now a capture left two steps in the undo list, one of which did nothing when undone, and
  the mode stayed Capture
- Showing, moving and turning the root marker are no longer steps in the undo list
- The picture stays where it is on the screen when it is captured. The room that is kept around a drawing, so
  that the view can be scrolled a little beyond it, now also surrounds a picture behind the drawing in every
  mode, and it is changed without moving what is seen
- Not solved: in a drawing in which all edges are dashed, nodes are often not found; a support value that
  touches a line can still leave a short extra edge; and a network with many crossing edges of one colour
  still has a node at each crossing
